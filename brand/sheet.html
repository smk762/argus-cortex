Vesica iris

The eye outline is a true vesica piscis, the lens formed by two overlapping circles (r 11.82, centres 8.64 apart). Inside is a compound iris of seven eyes in the Seed of Life layout. Six are ink, and the centre one carries the colour. Every package mark keeps a single eye. The suite holds them all.

Many eyes, not one

Argus Panoptes had a hundred eyes, so the suite mark holds many and each package holds one. The red eye in each candidate is the one that carries the colour. Pick one or two to refine, and the bowl of the wordmark's a will take on the winner's interior.

Eyes from older traditions

A due-diligence pass through mythological and esoteric eye symbols. Where the frame allows, each one carries the compound iris, so it's judged against the current pick rather than starting from zero.

Considered and left out

Hamsa and the ankh are active religious symbols, and a dev-tool logo would be borrowing them. Odin's eye is one eye given up for wisdom, so it's the cyclops problem again. Indra Sahasraksha, the thousand-eyed god, is a better story than an image, and it can go in the README next to Argus Panoptes. The Seed of Life works the same way: the compound iris already uses its seven-circle layout, so it's a construction note, not a separate mark.

Construction

Rules every mark follows

  • Grid24 × 24 units with 2 units of padding. Live area is 20 × 20, and optical overshoot is allowed for round forms.
  • Stroke2 units, round caps and joins, never scaled separately from the mark.
  • PupilOne filled circle, r 2.25, in the stage colour. Keep at least 0.75 units of clear space between it and any stroke.
  • CornersRectangles use r 1.5 (small) or r 2–2.5 (frames). No sharp outer corners except the mountain and the funnel's spout.
  • MonoThe pupil may take the ink colour in one-colour use. The mark still reads, because the dot keeps its position.
  • Min size16 px. At 16 px the stroke lands at 1.33 px. A pixel-hinted 16 px set is a later step if favicons look soft.

Pixel-drawn favicon

Below 24 px the vector mark's seven dots merge into a grey disc. This version is drawn pixel by pixel on a 16 × 16 grid: a 1 px vesica, seven 2 × 2 px eyes with 1 px gutters, and a red centre. Hand-lettered "argus"
